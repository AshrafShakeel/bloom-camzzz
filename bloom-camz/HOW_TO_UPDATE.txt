BLOOM CAMZ — AB DAILY UPDATE SIRF data.js SE
================================================

1) NEW CAMERA ADD KARNA
-----------------------
- Camera images images/products/ folder mein rakho.
- data.js kholo.
- Neeche 3 "UPCOMING CAMERA SLOT" already diye hue hain.
- Kisi slot mein:
  id, name, meta, shortDescription, description, cardImage, images, specs fill karo.
- isActive: true kar do.
- launchAt mein Pakistan date/time do, example:
  launchAt: '2026-09-15T18:00:00+05:00'
- announceOnLaunch: true rehne do.

RESULT:
- Launch time se PEHLE camera Collection mein nahi dikhega.
- Coming Soon section automatically usko count karega.
- Launch time hote hi camera automatically Collection mein aa jayega.
- User ki first visit after launch par professional "New cameras are live" popup aayega.
- Popup har browser par us drop ke liye sirf ek baar aayega.
