Bloom Camz UI Update
====================

1. Comparison section is collapsed by default.
   - Click "Open camera comparison" to expand it.
   - The panel slides down with a smooth animation.
   - Click again to collapse it.

2. Header navigation now has an animated active indicator.
   - As the visitor scrolls, the current menu item becomes active.
   - On desktop, the plum underline slides from one menu item to the next.
   - On mobile, the active menu item gets a themed left accent/background.

No product/data format changes were required.
