the story

Digital, but make it nostalgic

Bloom Camz started as a small closet of childhood point-and-shoots — the kind with a wrist strap, a pop-up flash, and photos that come out just a little bit soft. We clean each one up, test the battery and card slot, and thread on a tiny charm before it finds a new home.

No two cameras are quite the same, so the collection changes often. Once it's gone, it's gone — until the next little bloom of stock arrives.
